Identity Tools

Tools for identity work

Utilities I reach for when troubleshooting SSO, tokens and Active Directory. They run entirely in your browser: tokens, assertions and values you paste are never sent to a server.

No backend, no analytics, network requests blocked by Content-Security-Policy

JWT decoder & verifier

Header, claims and validity window of an id_token or access token, with signature check against a secret, PEM, certificate or JWKS.

OIDCOAuth 2.0Entra ID

SAML decoder

SAMLRequest and SAMLResponse from the POST or Redirect binding: issuer, NameID, conditions, audience, attributes and signature algorithms.

SAML 2.0SSO

userAccountControl calculator

Decode or build the flags of an AD account, see which ones are a security concern, and get the LDAP filter to find them.

Active DirectoryLDAP

AD timestamp converter

FILETIME, Generalized Time and Unix epoch in both directions, AD intervals like maxPwdAge, and LDAP filters for stale accounts.

Active DirectoryLDAP
