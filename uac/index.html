    Good to know

    • LOCKOUT and PASSWORD_EXPIRED are not kept up to date in userAccountControl. Read msDS-User-Account-Control-Computed or lockoutTime / pwdLastSet instead.
    • PASSWD_CANT_CHANGE is enforced by an ACE on the object, not by this bit: setting it alone does nothing.
    • The OID 1.2.840.113556.1.4.803 is the bitwise AND matching rule (all bits must be set); 1.2.840.113556.1.4.804 is bitwise OR (any bit).
